One topic. A few minutes.
Then have a go.

Short, simple videos that explain one Maths or English idea at a time, with practice questions and answers underneath.

Who is it for?

Students who find Maths or English hard, have gaps in the basics, or just need to see it explained again, slowly and clearly.

It works for catch-up and intervention groups, homework, revision, or as a starter in class.

What makes it different?

Each video covers one idea in about 1 to 3 minutes. Pictures come first, the method comes second.

Every video has subtitles, plain language and no distractions.

How to use it

  1. Pick a topicOpen a row on the Maths or English page.
  2. Read the ideaA short explanation sits next to the video.
  3. Watch the videoPause it and replay it as often as you need.
  4. Try the questionsThen click Show answers to check.
